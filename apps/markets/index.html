03 / IN APP

Using the application

  1. 01

    Open your watchlist and select a company. Keep the quote, data timestamp, and research in one place.

  2. 02

    Inspect the report beside related reporting and video analysis. Distinguish reported facts from interpretations.

  3. 03

    Save a question, set an alert, or prepare a research note. Any available trading flow shows an explicit order review.

04 / APP CARD

Using the desktop App Card

  1. 01

    A desktop card tells you what changed, why it relates to your watchlist, and where to inspect the sources.

  2. 02

    Save it, open the research, or tune the alert. A research alert does not place an order.

05 / CONTEXT

How the context connects

Only the sources you have connected and allowed

Watchlist & market data

Use your selected assets and the actual freshness of the available data.

Reports, news & video

Bring primary material and commentary together while keeping their origins distinct.

Research notes

Compare the update with questions and assumptions you previously saved.

06 / CONTROL

What you authorize

Define your alerts and connected accounts. A trade requires a separate review of instrument, direction, quantity, and price conditions; research authorization does not authorize execution.

07 / TIMING

When the agent works

Reports and chosen alert conditions trigger research. Less urgent background reading can wait for the next planned review.
