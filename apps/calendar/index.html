03 / IN APP

Using the application

  1. 01

    Open the day or week view. Existing events keep their familiar positions and controls.

  2. 02

    Select the changed meeting to compare old and new details, see the source, and inspect the conflict.

  3. 03

    Choose an alternative or accept the change. A route, preparation notes, and a reply can be embedded below the event.

04 / APP CARD

Using the desktop App Card

  1. 01

    A rescheduling card shows both appointments and offers concrete alternatives, without requiring the full calendar.

  2. 02

    A later preparation or departure card refers back to the confirmed event rather than asking you to rebuild the plan.

05 / CONTEXT

How the context connects

Only the sources you have connected and allowed

Mail & messages

Use the invitation and later updates as the event’s provenance.

Weather & maps

Evaluate conditions at the event location and allow time to travel.

Documents

Bring relevant meeting material into the event when you have granted access.

06 / CONTROL

What you authorize

Show conflicts before changing commitments. Sending a counterproposal and editing a personal event are separate choices.

07 / TIMING

When the agent works

Invitations, schedule changes, and preparation times trigger the relevant card. Repeated updates modify the same event.
